Privacy & safety

Helpful without pretending to know everything.

Does Shift save my information?

By default, Shift stores your life threads only in this browser on this device. If you explicitly sign in and enable cloud sync, Shift will also copy your state to your dedicated cloud account so it can follow you across devices.

Can I move my information to another device?

Use “Export my data” in My life to download your Shift data as a JSON file. You can import that file on another device. Treat the export as private because it can contain sensitive information.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. “Clear this device” removes all locally stored Shift threads from this browser.

Is Shift therapy or psychological treatment?

No. Shift helps organize a difficult situation and surface practical next steps. It does not diagnose mental-health conditions, provide psychotherapy, or replace a licensed professional.

Is Shift medical, legal, financial, or immigration advice?

No. Shift is a planning and life-navigation tool. For high-stakes decisions, verify requirements with official sources or a qualified professional.

What if something feels urgent or dangerous?

Shift is not an emergency service. If someone may be in immediate danger or needs urgent medical or mental-health care, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service or qualified professional.
